# EU AI Act Approval Pack — Private Answer Key

## Case 1
### Client brief
A fashion retailer is rolling out a new internal workforce dashboard for managers. The dashboard combines weekly attendance, meeting activity, and manager notes with a score that is meant to indicate how engaged each employee or team appears during internal video briefings. The company wants to use the score to identify where coaching, support, or intervention may be needed.

### Intended category
Prohibited

### Why
This is a prohibited use because it infers emotional or engagement states from employees' facial expressions and voice patterns in the workplace, and it is used to influence managerial decisions about support or intervention.

## Case 2
### Client brief
A recruitment agency handles high volumes of hospitality applications. It wants to process incoming CVs, written application answers, and prior employment history through an AI ranking model so it can prioritise candidates for interviews. Recruiters generally interview applicants above a threshold, but they can review and override the ranking before making a final decision.

### Intended category
High-risk

### Why
This is high-risk because the system affects access to employment by ranking applicants for hiring decisions and is therefore within the employment-focused Annex III risk area.

## Case 3
### Client brief
An online beauty retailer wants to launch a conversational shopping assistant for customers. Shoppers can ask about skin concerns, preferences, product categories, and routines, and the assistant recommends products and suggested skincare plans. Customers can also escalate to a human support agent at any time. The assistant should feel natural, helpful, and personalised.

### Intended category
Limited risk / transparency

### Why
This is a limited-risk AI use because the system interacts directly with users and mainly triggers transparency obligations, not a high-risk employment or essential-service decision.

## Case 4
### Client brief
A photography studio wants to sort uploaded images into folders such as portraits, interiors, products, and landscapes. The AI groups images automatically, and the photographer reviews the results and reorganises folders manually if needed. The output is used for internal image organisation and does not drive access to employment, credit, public services, education, justice, or other consequential decisions.

### Intended category
Minimal risk

### Why
This is minimal risk because the system is used for image categorisation and human review, and it does not affect protected access to significant rights or services.
